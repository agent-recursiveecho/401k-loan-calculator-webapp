# Calculation methodology
- Employee deferral per paycheck = eligible gross × (pre-tax % + Roth %) / 100.
- Simplified employer match = eligible gross × min(employee %, match cap %) / 100 × employer match rate / 100.
- Nominal biweekly interest = APR / 26; standard level payment = P*r/(1-(1+r)^(-N)). Final payment clears the rounding balance.
- Estimated new take-home = current take-home - increased pre-tax deferral - increased Roth deferral + pre-tax increase × estimated marginal income tax rate.
- Cash changes: loan proceeds are counted once, loan repayments and rent are cash outflows; loan-funded rent does not generate a second income entry.
- Same-day event ordering is only a simulation, not a bank settlement prediction.
